## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- **Reviewing PRs** — Provide timely, constructive feedback on contributions
- **Responding to Issues** — Triage issues, answer questions, guide discussions
- **Guiding Evolution** — Shape the direction of the specification
- **Ensuring Quality** — Maintain backwards compatibility and specification clarity
- **Community Health** — Enforce the Code of Conduct, foster inclusive participation
